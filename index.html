Assembly Guide

1. Fold top to bottom

Start with the A4 sheet horizontal. Fold the top row down onto the bottom row and crease it.

2. Rotate, then fold twice

Rotate the paper to vertical. Fold it in half, then fold again so every page panel gets a crease.

3. Unfold once, then cut

Keep the paper folded horizontally. Cut the middle line between pages 1 and 6; pages 2 and 5 behind it get cut too.

4. Open, refold, then push

Open the sheet, fold horizontally again so the two rows become one long strip, then push both ends together. The slit opens into a diamond; fold the loose parts around until page 1 is the cover and the pages flip in order.
